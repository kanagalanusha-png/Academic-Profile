# Dr. Anusha Kanagala
### Management Faculty | Supply Chain Management | Marketing

Welcome to my academic portfolio. I am a management educator and researcher with experience teaching postgraduate and undergraduate learners. My academic interests span supply chain management, logistics, operations and lean management, marketing, customer relationship management, and technology-enabled business practices.

## About
- Ph.D. in Management, Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University, Kakinada (2017)
- MBA, Acharya Nagarjuna University (2009)
- B.Pharm, Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University, Hyderabad (2007)
- Management teaching experience since 2009
- UGC-NET and AP-SET qualified in Management (2012)
- UGC Junior Research Fellowship (2012)
